ClipForge Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

What we collect from TikTok: your TikTok account ID, display name, username and profile picture, and the access tokens TikTok issues when you connect. Nothing else.

Why: only to show which account is connected and to upload the videos you choose to that account.

Where it is kept: on the operator's own computer. Access tokens are stored encrypted. Data is never sold, shared or used for advertising.

How long: until you disconnect the account. Disconnecting deletes the stored tokens and asks TikTok to revoke them.

Your choices: disconnect in ClipForge, or remove ClipForge in TikTok → Settings → Security → Manage app permissions. Email shalomlichi@yahoo.com to have any remaining data deleted.

Children: ClipForge is not for anyone under 18.

Changes: updates are posted on this page with a new date.
